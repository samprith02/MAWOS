## Why idle gaps reach zero

Idle gaps come out at 0.00 ± 0.00. **That is mostly construction, not search.** The greedy seed lays each section out as periods 0..load−1 on every day, which is gap-free and late-start-free before a single annealing step runs; gaps only appear where a teacher conflict forces an overflow, and the objective then penalises them more heavily than anything else (weight 5.0). A solver designed to produce compact days producing compact days is not a discovery.

The number that is not built in is the **distance to the floor**: 8.2 points, made up of 0.90 late starts, 0.20 subject repeats and 3.90 teacher gaps that the search could not remove. Those are the residual, and they are where a better solver would show up.

**A trade-off the objective does not price.** Compaction lengthens the longest unbroken run of classes from 2.63 to 3.60 periods. With 18 periods over five gap-free days that is arithmetic, not a choice — but no term in the frozen objective charges for it, so if four classes back-to-back is worse than one mid-morning gap, this objective cannot say so.

## What this is and is not

Simulated annealing for timetabling is decades old and the plan (§4.4) retracted scheduling as a contribution. This is applied engineering with a measured before/after: the claim is that v2 had no objective function at all, that adding one fixes the two defects users actually complained about, and that it costs 12× the solve time to do so — once, at generation.
